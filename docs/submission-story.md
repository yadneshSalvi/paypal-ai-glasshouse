# Glasshouse

**Rewind the business. Review the evidence. Approve the exact remedy.**

Glasshouse is a commerce incident room for a PayPal business. A late-delivery signal becomes a shared investigation: the Commerce River shows when it emerged, the Time Dial reconstructs what was known, and the Evidence Lens connects affected orders to their original records. AI can investigate and build a useful view. The merchant reviews the proposed actions and can inspect what actually happened afterward.

[Open the qualified live application](https://paypal-ai-glasshouse.vercel.app).

[Watch the 2-minute-30-second demonstration](https://youtu.be/49wbJMi8S1I). Its chapters show retained native application states: a 600-order Sample investigation, a separate recorded AI investigation, a controlled correction from 63 to 54 affected orders, and the dated original PayPal Sandbox recovery. Chapter labels keep those scopes clear.

## The walkthrough

1. Rewind the Commerce River with the Time Dial. All six native instruments keep the same source and knowledge cutoff.
2. Select the Pacific Northwest cohort and follow the same records through the map, evidence and precise data views.
3. Inspect a recorded Investigator handoff and the native AG Studio view it created. Open its sources and counterpoint.
4. Read the nine fixture delivery updates behind the 63-to-54 revision. The earlier proposal remains visible as stale history.
5. Inspect the original exact recovery manifest, its approval, individual operation receipts and saved report.

The native source-review successor also offers searchable margin and checkout records, original evidence drill-through and complete downloads. Its mobile dialogs passed native containment and keyboard review. This local successor is separate from the deployed release.

## How AI uses PayPal

| Role | Allowed behavior | Execution boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investigator | Inspect admitted source records and owned provider observations; save a sourced hypothesis and counterpoint | Read access; it cannot approve a payment |
| Planner | Compare policy branches and prepare an exact proposal | The merchant must separately review the manifest |
| Operator | Follow an explicitly approved, current recovery handoff in the local successor | Current membership, original resources, manifest hash, expiry and durable operation identity are checked before dispatch |
| Writer | Summarize stored evidence and actual operation outcomes | Open cases and unknown results remain visible |

## Release boundaries

The live application remains the qualified `6bd66c6` release. Local filmed successors have their own source/build and native review records. The larger commercial successor implements persistent incident cases, hypotheses, operational learning, merchant admission, grounded facts and guarded invoice/subscription/storefront adapters; it has passing domain and UI controls but has not received integrated production, native or external-provider qualification. It is excluded from the live release and the film's execution claims.

The historical model comparison separates mechanical protocol success from automated semantic adequacy: OpenAI 16/16 and 13/16 respectively; Gemini 12/16 and 1/16. This is an automated assessment, not a human causal validation. Prospective grounded-output fixes are implemented locally; their new actual-model evaluation was not run.

No seller study, external participant quote, full screen-reader/200% zoom audit, authenticated webhook delivery qualification, or fresh full merchant rehearsal is claimed.
